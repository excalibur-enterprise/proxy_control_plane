Finalize your Excalibur subscription

Click the button below to complete your purchase. We will redeem the marketplace token, sign a license bundle, and email you a download link within ~5 minutes.

What happens next

  1. This page sends your one-time marketplace token to our GitHub Actions fulfillment workflow via repository_dispatch.
  2. The workflow calls Microsoft's SaaS Fulfillment v2 API to resolve and activate your subscription.
  3. It signs a license bundle with our offline operational key and commits it to the public excalibur-enterprise/proxy_control_plane repo under bundles/<opaque-id>.json.
  4. Our static entitlements endpoint serves it within seconds; your proxy auto-fetches on its next 24-hour cycle.

You can verify the resulting bundle locally with:

excalibur-ctl license verify ./bundle.json
